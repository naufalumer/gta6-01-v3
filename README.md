AZURA BAY: Neon Tide is an open-world crime game set in a bright coastal city with a turquoise bay, a white-sand beach, a neon downtown strip, a working container port, hillside villas, and an old cannery quarter. You play Rico Vane, a driver who comes back to the city and builds a crew: Lena plans the jobs, Tank handles the muscle, and Mira drives and hacks. Together they run six linked story missions against Victor Kane and the Harbor Syndicate, from a first drop in Sundial Plaza to a final street showdown with Kane's convoy.

The city is split into five named districts, and each one keeps its own opinion of you: Seaglass Beach, Sundial Square, Saltflats Port, Palm Heights, and Old Cannery. How a district sees you changes what missions pay there, what shops charge, and how quickly the police show up. The streets stay busy with around 26 traffic cars and around 64 pedestrians who walk, cross roads, chat, run from trouble, and climb into cars. Eighteen parked cars can be stolen, and there is also a rideable motorcycle, a flyable helicopter with two helipads, and a jetpack with thrust and fuel to manage.

Heat builds on a five-star ladder. At one star, foot cops chase you down. Higher stars bring patrol cars, roadblocks, PIT maneuvers, and finally a helicopter, and the HUD keeps a live patrol list showing each unit's distance and tactic. You fight back with a pistol, an SMG, and a shotgun, and drifting leaves skid marks and smoke behind you while paying a cash bonus. Between missions there is plenty to do: fish at Pier 9 and sell the catch, shoot hoops at Seaglass Court, try the casino slots, race through checkpoints, run courier jobs, hunt hidden packages, and spend cash at Tideworks Customs on repairs, resprays, tunes, armor, nitro, and weapons.

The HUD packs in a mission card, wanted stars, cash, respect, a circular minimap with the current district, a speedometer with health, armor, and nitro bars, and a radio with three original hip-hop stations that gets more intense during chases. A full day cycle moves through morning, noon, dusk, and night, each with its own palette, and every sound, from sirens and engines to the radio, is synthesized live with WebAudio. The game runs offline in the browser with no build step, and Three.js is vendored in the repo.

Controls: WASD or arrows to move and drive, mouse to look and aim, click to fire, F to enter or exit a vehicle, E to interact, Space for handbrake and jump, Shift for nitro and sprint, 1/2/3 to switch weapons, R to reload, Q to switch radio stations, M for the map, Esc to pause. On a phone you get a left joystick plus FIRE, JUMP, ENTER, and USE buttons.
