An open-world crime game in the spirit of GTA 6, built from scratch with original characters, places, and music. No Rockstar IP and no borrowed assets: every street, face, and song in Azura Bay is our own.

**Play it now:** https://naufalumer.github.io/gta6-01-v3/game/

Steal cars, outrun the law, run six story missions, and take the city back from the Harbor Syndicate, all in your browser.

## The city

Azura Bay is a sun-soaked coastal city split into five districts, each with its own look, traffic, and trouble:

- **Seaglass Beach:** boardwalk, sand, and the Pier 9 fishing spot.
- **Sundial Square:** downtown towers, shops, and the busiest streets in town.
- **Saltflats Port:** docks, warehouses, and the Harbor Syndicate's turf.
- **Palm Heights:** hillside homes, wide views, and quiet roads that do not stay quiet.
- **Old Cannery:** rusted factories, back alleys, and the best hiding spots in the city.

## The story

Rico Vane comes home to Azura Bay with a debt to pay and a crew to rebuild. Lena plans the jobs, Tank drives anything with wheels, and Mira keeps the crew one step ahead of the law. Standing in their way is Victor Kane and the Harbor Syndicate, who own the port and want the whole city next.

Six chained missions carry the story from a small beachfront score to an all-out war for the docks. Each mission opens the next, and only the full chain takes Kane down.

## The law

Trouble brings a wanted level of one to five stars. Patrol cars tail you and box you in at one and two stars, roadblocks and PIT moves start at three, and the helicopter joins the chase at four and five.

Break line of sight to lose them, or get busted and lose your cash.

## A living city

- Around 26 AI cars share the roads with around 64 pedestrians across all five districts.
- 18 parked cars can be stolen. Take one and it is yours until the law takes it back.
- Pistol, SMG, and shotgun, each with its own feel, plus a weapon shop to stock up.
- Tideworks Customs garage for tuning your ride, and casino slots for risking your mission cash.
- Motorbike, jetpack, and helicopter (two helipads across the city) for when the road is not enough.
- Fishing at Pier 9 and basketball at Seaglass Court when the crew needs a break.
- Drifting leaves skid marks, tire smoke, and a screech, and a clean drift pays a cash bonus.

## HUD and menus

Wanted stars, cash, respect, minimap, speedometer, health and armor, mission tracker, radio display, and dialog prompts are all on screen. Title, pause, busted, and wasted screens frame every run, and progress saves to localStorage so the city waits for you.

Press **M** for the full map: all five districts, mission markers, and your stats at a glance.

Cash and respect earned from missions, drifts, and side activities feed straight back into cars, guns, and garage tunes.

## Sound and light

- A full WebAudio synth soundtrack, built in code with no audio files.
- Three original hip-hop radio stations. Press **Q** to switch while you drive.
- A full day cycle: morning, day, dusk, and night, each with its own palette.
- Cinematic grade with ACES tone mapping and a vignette, so every sunset looks like a movie still.

## Controls

- **WASD / Arrows:** move and drive. **Mouse:** aim. **Click:** shoot.
- **F:** enter or exit a vehicle. **E:** interact. **Space:** handbrake, jump, jetpack, or climb in a helicopter.
- **Shift:** nitro or sprint. **1 / 2 / 3:** pistol, SMG, shotgun. **R:** reload.
- **Q:** switch radio station. **M:** map. **Esc:** pause.
- **Touch:** virtual joystick plus on-screen FIRE, JUMP, ENTER, and USE buttons.

## Run it locally

Serve the `game/` folder with any static file server and open `index.html`. No build step, no install, no server code: the whole game runs in the browser from its own files.
