A sun-soaked coastal open-world crime game, built as original IP. Azura Bay is a turquoise bay city where a white-sand beachfront meets a pastel art-deco downtown, a working container port, hillside villas and a weathered old town. It looks like a holiday postcard and plays like a heist movie.

## The city

Five districts make up the map, each with its own feel:

- **Seaglass Beach** — sand, surf and the basketball court
- **Sundial Square** — the pastel heart of downtown, home to Neon Row
- **Saltflats Port** — containers, cranes and the Harbor Syndicate's turf
- **Palm Heights** — hillside villas above the bay
- **Old Cannery** — the rougher, older side of town

A full map screen (press **M**) shows all five districts plus your stats, so you can plan a route before you make a run for it.

## The story

Six chained missions follow Rico Vane and his crew — Lena, Tank and Mira — as they take on Victor Kane and the Harbor Syndicate. The jobs escalate from street level to a full confrontation, with side jobs scattered around the city for extra cash and respect between story beats.

## Heat

The police ladder runs from 1 to 5 stars. Foot cops give way to patrol cars, then roadblocks, then a helicopter that will try PIT manoeuvres to spin you out. Lose the heat, or get busted — or wasted. The choice is usually yours, right up until it is not.

## Driving and the streets

About 26 AI traffic cars and 64 pedestrians keep the streets alive, and 18 parked cars are stealable if you fancy an upgrade in transport. Street racing is a core part of the game: drift through corners and you get smoke, skid marks, a screech and a drift cash bonus for doing it well.

There is also a rideable motorcycle, a flyable helicopter from two helipads, and a jetpack for when the road is not enough. Space is your handbrake on the ground, your jump on foot, your jetpack thrust in the air, and your climb in the helicopter.

## Combat and gear

Pistol, SMG and shotgun, switchable with **1 / 2 / 3**, reload with **R**. The HUD keeps everything in view: wanted stars, cash, respect, a patrol list, minimap, speedometer, health and armor, mission tracker, radio and a dialogue box.

Spend your earnings at Tideworks Customs (the garage, for upgrades) and the gun shop, or risk it all on casino slots.

## Life in the bay

Not everything is a chase. You can fish at Pier 9, shoot hoops at Seaglass Court, and cruise with the radio on. Three original hip-hop stations are built in with fully synthesised WebAudio sound — press **Q** to switch stations.

The day cycles through morning, noon, dusk and night, each with its own palette and cinematic colour grading, so the same street looks completely different at 2pm and 2am.

## Menus and saves

Title, pause, busted and wasted screens are all in, and progress saves to localStorage so you can pick up where you left off.

## Controls

| Action | Key |
| --- | --- |
| Drive / move | WASD or arrow keys |
| Look / aim | Mouse |
| Fire | Click |
| Enter / exit vehicle | F |
| Interact | E |
| Handbrake / jump / jetpack / heli climb | Space |
| Nitro / sprint | Shift |
| Switch weapon | 1 / 2 / 3 |
| Reload | R |
| Radio station | Q |
| Map | M |
| Pause | Esc |

On mobile, a touch joystick plus FIRE / JUMP / ENTER / USE buttons cover the same actions.
